What is this long string of letters and numbers?

It is a fingerprint of the web address above. The proper name is a SHA-256 hash, sometimes called a checksum.

A hash works like a blender for text. Put in the exact same text and you always get the exact same 64 characters out. Change even one letter, one dot, or add a space, and you get a completely different result. You also can't run it backwards to turn the fingerprint into the address.

So the fingerprint lets you check that the address on this page hasn't been changed. Nobody has to take our word for it, because anyone can make the fingerprint themselves and compare.

If the fingerprints match, you know:
  • The address you checked is exactly, character for character, the one we published.
  • Nobody has swapped in a look-alike address, such as commbank-login.com or cornmbank.com.au (an "rn" in place of the "m").
A match does not tell you:
  • That this page itself is genuine. If someone copied this whole page and changed both the address and the fingerprint, they would still match each other. That is why you should compare against a fingerprint from somewhere else, like a printout you were given or a trusted friend's copy.
  • That your computer or phone is free of viruses.
  • That CommBank hasn't moved its login page since we checked it.

Check it on another website

Don't want to trust this page's own checker? Use someone else's. These are free, well-known tools run by other people, so they're independent of us.

  1. Press Copy address at the top of this page.
  2. Open one of the sites below and paste the address into its box. Make sure there's no space or blank line before or after it.
  3. Choose SHA-256 if it asks. (Not SHA-1, SHA-512 or MD5. Those are different.)
  4. Compare its answer with our fingerprint. It's enough to check the first 6 and last 6 characters. Capital or small letters don't matter.

    Pasting a bank's web address into these sites is harmless, because it's public information. Never paste your password, card number or anything private into them.
